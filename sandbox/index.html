      What this prototype tests
      • Queue order is the skill expression. Like Seven Knights, you choose who fires first. Stun with Brann or Sorrel before the enemy phase and that enemy loses its turn.
      • Position is a resource. Like Enchanted Arms, each side has its own grid (4×4 by default; 3×3 and 4 wide × 3 deep under Tuning). Tanks in front block melee, and deep rows soak less ranged damage. Repositioning costs a turn.
      • Shapes matter. Lane, row, cross and full-grid attacks reward stacking enemies and punish you for clumping.
      • Systems before characters. The engine supports tile effects, round-scoped team buffs, chain-neutral moves, per-unit stacks and skills that ignore falloff. No hero uses them yet; the checks run them on a neutral test unit. The v0.2 rule proposals sit behind toggles under Tuning.
      • Four elements. Water douses Fire, Fire consumes Wind, Wind erodes Earth, Earth dams Water; opposites are neutral. Badges use minimal solid glyphs: flame, drop, peaks, gusts. Augments are bound spirits (Salamander, Undine, Gnome, Sylph): they rename the skill and give the card an aura, and a 4★ gets a prismatic frame. The v0.1 triangle stays under Tuning for the reference fights.
      • Cards, not pulls. Every copy of a hero is a card with its own rolled stats. Two cards of the same star level merge into one a star higher, up to 3★; an Ashcrown takes a 3★ to 4★. A card holds one augment per star. Augments swap freely until a merge sets them, and fodder augments that don't fit go back to the loose pile.
      • Not yet in: gear, speed-based initiative for your side, a real level-up flow, the run map. Those sit on top once the core loop feels right.
