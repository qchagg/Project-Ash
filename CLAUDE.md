# Working on Project Ash

- The game is the Godot 4.7 project in `game/`, and it is the source of truth for every rule and screen. Build new work there directly; rules live in `game/core/`. Comments and naming follow GDScript style (snake_case, tabs, `##` doc comments).
- Build every screen from `game/ui/forge.gd`: its palette, Alegreya Sans SC set in lowercase (even small caps), `art/icons/ui/` glyphs, and the button roles (studs commit, at most one per screen; ember is everyday; links move). Don't style controls inline.
- Run `tools/godot-checks.sh` before committing Godot changes; every check must pass. Add a check for each new rule. The two reference fights replay in these checks and must not change.
- After changing the game, run `tools/godot-export-web.sh` and republish `build/web/index.html` (with its files) to https://claude.ai/artifact/9BKdbsQVseK1YN7DhY1CKw. `build/` is not committed.
- The same game also exports wide: `tools/godot-export-web.sh landscape` (the "landscape" feature turns the viewport to 1560×720; `Forge.wide()` is true) into `build/web-landscape/`, published at https://claude.ai/artifact/PLCWSdFwiUZ6o9GXhV1Avn. Keep both layouts working when you change a screen. The two builds are two directions: the phone stacks a hero's pages (HeroPager); the landscape build shows a roster of cards with a small switch to standing models, and HeroWide (ui/hero_wide.gd) lays a hero out with the list on the left, the character in the middle and tabs on the right. The landscape battle board uses battle sprites (Figure in ui/figure.gd, Stand in ui/stand.gd, BattleFigure on the battle board). The landscape board is a painted scene (`BattleScreen._wide_scene`): the road's painting and its Weather fill the screen with no nav, the two grids lie on a perspective floor (`BattleScreen.floor_quad`), fighters are `BattleFigure.TALL` high with a slim bar at their feet, and the controls stay small at the edges, the skills being `Forge.orb` buttons. `Backdrop.GROUNDS` stays with the phone. Sprites and their sizes are `Data.SPRITES`; new sprite art goes through `tools/sprite-cutout.py` and imports lossy.
- The app owns the nav (Heroes · Expedition · Town · Menu); screens set `tab` and fill their body. The game opens on `MainMenu` (ui/main_menu.gd) under the title: home features one road at a time on its painting, with one commit and plain links along the foot instead of the nav. `TravelLog` (ui/travel_log.gd) pays `Town.LOG` milestones for wardens felled. On a wide screen Heroes is tall strips with side filters and Squad is a Battlefield-style line-up (`SquadScreen.LINE`), with formation one link away. Expedition rules: `design/expedition.md`; town: `design/town.md`. Playtest links ending `#event-boon` (camp, wayside, fight, warden, pick), `#town` or `#heroes` open straight there.
- Use `Rim` (ui/rim.gd) for animated borders: comet for what you can act on, flow for what is chosen, pulse for the commit.
- Work runs in lanes, each on its own branch and worktree, cut from `dev`: `lane/audio`, `lane/art`, `lane/road`, `lane/screens` (and `lane/combat`, `lane/world` when opened). After creating a worktree, run `bash tools/setup-fork.sh` in it, since licensed audio is not in git. A lane finishes on its branch with every check passing and tells Release; it never exports, publishes or merges itself. Only the Release session, in the main folder on `dev`, merges lanes, rebuilds generated files (`game/core/faces.gd` comes from organize-art, never a hand merge), watches the pack ceiling (16 MB), exports and publishes. Keep changes to shared files (`data.gd`, `app.gd`, `run_checks.gd`, this file) small.
- Licensed audio stays out of git and off the public repo: `game/audio/music/` and the pack sounds in `game/audio/sfx/` (Shapeforms and Free Fantasy; Shapeforms' maker has said a public repo counts as redistribution). Raw art in `art/_archive/restyled/` is also local only, so read it from the main folder.
- If Godot is missing in a fresh session, run `tools/setup-godot.sh`.
- The sandbox (`sandbox/index.html`, published at https://claude.ai/artifact/Ln4qNwpjb9wkEykWCy2ShL) is frozen as the record of the v0.1 rules and the two reference fights. Don't add new rules to it.
- Design decisions live in the Docs tracker (https://claude.ai/code/artifact/5425ff38-fc13-4ad2-99de-10ba5c00adea). Log decisions there, newest first in the decisions table.
- The user is learning French: a short French line with its English translation is welcome in replies.
- Art uploads land anywhere under `art/`. Run `python3 tools/organize-art.py`: it files them into `art/<character>/<character>-<what>` (old names like fox, ilse, leaf, rock map to the cast's names), keys `-face-` clips and stills on green (magenta for heroes who wear green) into `game/art/clips/` and `game/art/faces/`, builds `-body-` stills and loops into battle sprites and idle frame sheets (`tools/body-clip.py`, `Faces.BODIES` over `Data.SPRITES`), and regenerates `game/core/faces.gd`. Clips and idle sheets stay out of the game pack (it has a 16 MB ceiling) and ship beside the page in `clips/` and `sheets/` (`ui/clips.gd`); when republishing, leave unchanged big files (the engine, old clips) out of the publish, since one publish carries at most 64 MB; a hero's live face is `Bust` (ui/bust.gd), their lines are `lines` in Data. Then rebuild the Asset Viewer (`python3 tools/asset-viewer.py <dir>`) and republish it to https://claude.ai/artifact/9pYkCDzSwERRXUTKb5NBjr; its `notes` collection holds the user's on/off-model verdicts and requests (such as "archive"); act on any note without a `done` field, then add `done` saying what you did. Superseded art goes to `art/_archive/`.
- Sound goes through `Sfx.play("<cue>")` (ui/sfx.gd), which plays `game/audio/sfx/<cue>.ogg` or a numbered variation (`<cue>-2.ogg`…) at a slightly varied pitch. Today's files are synthetic placeholders from `tools/make-sfx.py`; when a bought pack lands (in `art/_packs/`), convert the chosen sounds to short mono Vorbis under the same cue names, and they replace the placeholders. Every cue the code plays must have a file (a check enforces it). Battle feel lives in BattleScreen's playback: a real hit-stop (`Engine.time_scale`), trauma shake, element-coloured sparks and slashes, recoil, the KO fall, and health bars with a lagging chunk (`hp_fx`).
- The City Atlas (https://claude.ai/artifact/NyPDCgSz2TGwhSjF9ar2kA) shows every home, city and ruin, and what is painted, playable and missing. Whenever a background, road or home changes, update the rows in `tools/city-atlas.py`, run `python3 tools/city-atlas.py <out.html>` and republish it. The user reads it to see what we have.
- `Sfx.MUTED` lists cues kept silent (the placeholder `tap` and `page`, which the user found grating).
- Hero voices go through `Voice.play(unit_key, "attack" | "hurt" | "fall")` (ui/voice.gd): `Voice.CAST` gives each hero an actor and pitch. Clips are 22 kHz WAVs in `game/audio/voice/` (with a `.gdignore`, so they stay out of the pack) and ship in `sheets/`. They are CC BY 4.0: keep the credit in the Archive's settings.
- Music goes through `Music.play("<track>")` (ui/music.gd), which crossfades looping tracks and follows the sound switch; the app picks the track with `app.music(slot)`. Each road's tracks are `music` in `Expedition.ROADS`, and an empty slot falls back through `Expedition.music_for` (the shared `MUSIC_DEFAULT`, then the road's map track). Choices are made on the Road Music board (https://claude.ai/artifact/Nod2YCoKMYeXhBW9tZfsBM, db collection `road-music`). The tracks are AlkaKrab loops, in `game/audio/music/`, named as on the board. That folder is gitignored and has a `.gdignore`: the licence forbids redistributing the files as they are, and asks permission for open-source games, and the repo is public, so never commit them. They ship beside the page in `music/` and load into user:// (a fresh checkout has none and plays silence; export needs them). A check keeps the folder and the road data in step, so remove a file when no road uses it.
- A body still with no idle loop is auto-rigged (`tools/auto-rig.py`, run by organize-art): from the cutout alone it finds the planted feet, head, chest and loose parts (braid, sleeves, hem), and writes a weight grid into `Faces.BODIES[...].rig`. Figure draws such a sprite as a mesh and moves it (breath, nod, weight shift, a lagging sway of loose parts); the motion constants are `RIG_*` in ui/figure.gd. A check keeps the feet planted. Rigid props (a staff, a sword) sway like cloth for now. Surya, Brann, Sorrel and Varuni's body clips are on hold (`HOLD_CLIPS` in organize-art) so the whole cast moves the same way; the clips stay in `art/`. Every sprite is stored facing left, the one default; the game mirrors heroes to face right and leaves foes facing left. A still uploaded facing right goes in `MIRROR` in organize-art, judged by eye from each new still — the check only proves no sprite carries its own direction, not that the art really faces left.
- Every hero now has one, so nobody is rigged today, but the rig stays for a new still with no loop. Painted backgrounds are `game/art/backgrounds/<art>.webp` at 1792 wide (raw files in `art/_archive/restyled/bg-*`). `Backdrop.ART` lists each home's backgrounds and a hero's page picks one each time it opens (`Backdrop.painted(home)`, a `Painted`, in both layouts). They stay out of the pack and ship in `sheets/` with the idle sheets, so run organize-art after adding one, for the stamp. `Weather` (ui/weather.gd) loops each background's air between it and the hero (snow, dust, petals, leaves, mist): every background needs an entry in `Weather.AIR`. `tools/glow-spots.py` finds each painting's lights, organize-art writes them into `Faces.GLOWS`, and Weather flickers them, so run organize-art after adding a background. `StatusFx` (ui/status_fx.gd) draws burn, stun, shield and guard on the fighters in battle. Battle idles made in Gamelabs arrive as transparent sheets: `art/<character>/<character>-sheet-idle.webp` with `<character>-sheet-idle.json` (`cols`, `rows`, `n`, `fps`, `lead` for the run-up frames that blend the loop's join, `key` if magenta). organize-art runs `tools/sheet-idle.py`, which clears leftover key colour, adds the soft dark edge, pins the feet, and makes both the sprite and the 560-tall idle sheet, so a sheet needs no still and no rig. A sheet is not a clip: it comes after `HOLD_CLIPS` and wins over stills and body clips even for a hero listed there, but it has no attack, so `<character>-body-attack.mp4` stays unused for that hero. Raw full bodies and their framed versions are in `art/_archive/restyled/`. MIRROR flips a sheet too; judge it from the sheet's own frames. A device keeps its sheets in user://, so organize-art writes `Faces.SHEETS_STAMP`, and when it changes `Clips.refresh_sheets` clears the kept ones; run organize-art after any sheet changes.
- HeroWide has two views: details and story show the portrait, while skills, equip and evolve show the hero as their animated battle sprite (`_shows_figure`). `crops.profile` ([x, y, height] in %) sets the portrait view, in the Portrait Crops tool's "hero profile" view (https://claude.ai/artifact/1P4uTEaZxBhzPGtoFFM9mq). A crop fills the room edge to edge. A hero whose portrait is cut off or too close gets a stand instead: `tools/hero-stand.gd` keys a raw full body into `game/art/stands/<hero>-stand.webp` (ships in `sheets/`), named by `stand` in their `portrait`; the profile crop is then of the stand.
- Expedition's road choice is a big view (`_landing` in ui/expedition_screen.gd): one road fills the screen, with next, back and a strip of thumbnails at the foot, and "all roads" opens the grid. `Backdrop.SCREEN_ART` holds paintings behind whole screens: `caravan` behind the Caravan tab (still `town` in the code) and `dream` behind Heroes.
- The title screen (ui/title_screen.gd) shows a random background from `Backdrop.all_art()`, so a background added to `Backdrop.ART` joins it with no other change. The art drifts slowly (`TitleScreen.pan`) under that background's own `Weather` with its haze off (`clear`); a check fails if a file in `game/art/backgrounds/` isn't listed. Playtest links skip it.
- A road's fights stand on a seamless ground: `Backdrop.GROUNDS` (road -> `art`, `shade`), files `game/art/grounds/ground-<art>.webp` from Gamelabs at 1024 square (`tileable`, top camera, the road's painting as reference; raw PNGs in `art/_archive/restyled/`). They tile at their own size behind the whole battle screen, ship in `sheets/` and load through `Clips.sheet`. Set `shade` so each lands near the same brightness. A road with no ground fights on plain ink. Run organize-art after changing one, for the stamp.
- Sound plays only while the game is in front: `game/web/shell.html` suspends the page's audio when the tab is hidden or loses focus, and it resumes where it stopped. It has to be the page, since a hidden tab stops the game's own loop.
- Every skill has an icon in `game/art/skills/<skill-name>.webp`, a white mask cut from the user's gold icon sheets by `tools/skill-icons.py` (raw sheets in `art/_archive/restyled/icons-*.png`; `ICONS` there maps a skill to its cell). `Forge.skill_icon` tints it with `Forge.ELEM` in a shader and passes `Forge.SKILL_SHINE` over it, and an augmented skill keeps its base skill's icon. A new skill needs a cell, or a check fails. The Game Icon Pack on the Desktop has no licence, so it must not ship or be committed.
